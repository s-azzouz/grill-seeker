About Grill Seeker

Helping you decide what to cook with what you already have.

Why it exists

Deciding what to cook is harder than cooking it. Grill Seeker takes the ingredients already in your kitchen and finds recipes that use them, so you spend less time scrolling and more time eating.

Who it is for

  • People who can't decide what to make tonight.
  • Anyone looking for new recipe ideas.
  • Parents cooking for their families who want variety.

How it works

  1. Add the ingredients you have, one at a time.
  2. Optionally pick a cuisine or a category.
  3. Choose Find recipes, or Surprise me for a random idea.

You can also explore the cuisines and the dietary guide to learn more before you cook.

Where the recipes come from

Recipe data comes from the Spoonacular API. Choosing View recipe on a card opens the full recipe on their website in a new tab.
